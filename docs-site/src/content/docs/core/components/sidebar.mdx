## When to use it

- On every back office page, fixed on the left at full height, with header type=backoffice to its right.
- Mark the page you are on as active, and only that one: the app currently always marks Dashboard active, which needs fixing.
- Keep the item order the same on every page, so people find things by position.

3 more lines in `use_when` in `src/components/SideBar/SideBar.intent.json` (items 4 to 6) are Figma's Best Practice lines, word for word. They are listed once, under "Best practice" below.

## Where it goes

- On the left of a back office page, with header type=backoffice to its right (the BackOfficePage story).

### Not built

Figma draws none of these, and `SideBar.tsx` and `SideBar.css` at `680282e` build none of them:

- a collapse state or toggle
- a small-screen layout
- arrow-key navigation (Tab, Shift+Tab and Enter work because each item is a native link)
- groups or submenus (the Figma Usage line says to group when there are more than 12 items)
- disabled items (the Figma best practice says to hide items the role cannot open instead)
- a skip link
- a per-item destination: `href` is optional and falls back to `"#"` ([`SideBar.tsx:76`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/680282ead001fb5128702575d313303134d3261e/src/components/SideBar/SideBar.tsx#L76)), so an item with no `href` goes nowhere
- the cap of 12 items: any length of `items` is rendered
- the rule that only one item is active: every item with `current` set is marked

The 2026-10-07 ruling names the first six as "Anything Figma does not draw and the component does not build" and leaves them to a human. It reads, as written: "Anything Figma does not draw and the component does not build: a collapse state, small-screen layout, arrow-key navigation, groups, disabled items, a skip link."

## Best practice

From the Figma documentation page (usage node `247:520`), as written. `SideBar.intent.json` carries these same lines as items 4 to 6 of `use_when`, word for word; they are listed here once.

- Use short labels of one to three words, such as "Team and roles".
- Show which organisation and how many properties the admin is looking at in the footer.
- Hide items the person's role can't open, instead of showing them disabled.

The reviewer computed 2.16:1 on the panel and 1.60:1 on the active fill, and the figures were checked again for this page against the built token values (#1d44ba on #161925, #1d44ba on #162d69); only on the hover fill does it reach 7.76:1.
