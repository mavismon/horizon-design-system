## When to use it

- To pick one option from two to five where each needs a line of detail, such as the rate ("Whole apartment · non refundable"), when to pay, or which saved card to use at checkout.
- Use the trailing text for the price of each option, such as "484.00 EUR" or "0.00 EUR now"; turn it off when options have no price, such as payment methods.
- Use state disabled for an option that exists but can't be picked right now, such as a sold-out rate, and say why in the description.

## Where it goes

- Stacked vertically in a radiogroup (the Group story, as in the Figma "Payment group").

## When not to use it

- For more than five options, or options with no detail: use a Dropdown.<br/>**Instead:** Dropdown
- To pick several options: use Checkboxes.<br/>**Instead:** Checkbox
- To switch a view, such as a time range: use Buttongroup type segmented.<br/>**Instead:** ButtonGroup

**Both Dropdown and RadioCard claim five options.** RadioCard's first "when to use" item says "To pick one option from two to five where each needs a line of detail, such as the rate ("Whole apartment · non refundable"), when to pay, or which saved card to use at checkout." and Dropdown's first says "To pick one value from a list of five or more, such as the property filter "All properties" on the back office calendar, or "Who" in the activity log filters." ([Dropdown](/core/components/dropdown/)).

From the [release review](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/a3b743e011ce30357cf48d43fd83c5526188d2a1/reports/RadioCard/release-review.md), under "Findings outside the gates", as written:

> Dropdown and RadioCard both claim five options. Dropdown says "five or more", RadioCard says "two to five". The boundary at exactly five is claimed by both. Check 6 passes on the literal test, and each intent points to the other. Owner: the designer.

## Best practice

From the Figma documentation page (usage node `212:49`), as written. These items aren't carried into `RadioCard.intent.json`, which has no field for them.

- Always preselect one card, usually the recommended or the current choice, such as the default card.
- Stack cards vertically with 8px between them, all the same width, and make the whole card clickable.
- Keep the title short and put conditions in the description, such as "free cancellation until 12 Sep".

## Accessibility

- The control is a native &lt;input type="radio"&gt;, so keyboard (Tab, arrow keys within a name group), focus and disabled behaviour are the browser's own; state="disabled" sets the real disabled attribute. [`src/components/RadioCard/RadioCard.tsx:34`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/c1052106ef1cc81c8a4da5f07f6c686858758716/src/components/RadioCard/RadioCard.tsx#L34)
- The whole card is one &lt;label&gt; around the input, so clicking anywhere on the card selects it and the title and description give it its accessible name. [`src/components/RadioCard/RadioCard.tsx:32`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/c1052106ef1cc81c8a4da5f07f6c686858758716/src/components/RadioCard/RadioCard.tsx#L32)
- The drawn radio is decorative (aria-hidden); checked state is carried by the native input. [`src/components/RadioCard/RadioCard.tsx:41`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/c1052106ef1cc81c8a4da5f07f6c686858758716/src/components/RadioCard/RadioCard.tsx#L41)
