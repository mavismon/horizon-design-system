## When to use it

- Use type dialog to confirm an action before it happens, such as "Remove Luis Correia?", or for a short task that must finish before going back, such as an invite.
- Use tone destructive when the action deletes or can't be undone; show what changes in the content rows, such as "Can be undone: No".
- Use type sheet on mobile for filters, pickers and short choices that slide up from the bottom.

3 more lines in `use_when` in `src/components/Modal/Modal.intent.json` (items 4 to 6) are Figma's Best Practice lines, word for word. They are listed once, under "Best practice" below.

## Where it goes

- Centred on a color/bg/overlay backdrop (the OnOverlay story); on screen, opened from a Button as a native modal dialog (the Open* stories).

## When not to use it

- For information people should read while they keep working: show it on the page, or in a toast.<br/>*The Figma page names no alternative.*
- For long forms or anything with more than one step: use a page with Stepper.<br/>**Instead:** Stepper
- Open a modal from another modal.<br/>*The Figma page names no alternative.*

**Open item: no alternative named for items 1 and 3.** Those items have an empty `instead` in `Modal.intent.json`. The Figma page names no Horizon component for them: "show it on the page, or in a toast" names a pattern, and "Open a modal from another modal" names none. This page shows "The Figma page names no alternative" and does not fill the gap.

From the [release review](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/141d9ba1e022de1c81ea3b283ea15885bc769d17/reports/Modal/release-review.md), under "Warnings", as written:

> C2: two dont_use_when entries have an empty instead: "For information people should read while they keep working: show it on the page, or in a toast." and "Open a modal from another modal." Figma names no Horizon component for either.

Treat it as guidance for the person using the component.

## Best practice

From the Figma documentation page (usage node `244:154`), as written. `Modal.intent.json` carries these same lines as items 4 to 6 of `use_when`, word for word; they are listed here once.

- Ask a question in the title and name the thing, such as "Remove Luis Correia?", and put the result of the action on the main button, such as "Remove admin", not "OK".
- Always give a way out: Cancel, the close ×, the Escape key and a click on the backdrop.
- Place the dialog in the centre on a color/bg/overlay backdrop; place the sheet at the bottom, full width.

## Accessibility

- The root is a native &lt;dialog&gt;, so it has the dialog role; on screen it is opened with showModal(), which makes the rest of the page inert, keeps focus inside and returns it to the opener on close. [`src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx:115`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/b9ffb76f7673fe33ddc04617d587557e93084f96/src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx#L115)
- The dialog is named by its title (aria-labelledby) and described by its message (aria-describedby, only when a message exists). [`src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx:130`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/b9ffb76f7673fe33ddc04617d587557e93084f96/src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx#L130)
- Escape is cancelled and handed to onClose, so the owner decides when the dialog closes. [`src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx:132`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/b9ffb76f7673fe33ddc04617d587557e93084f96/src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx#L132)
- A click on the backdrop calls onClose; the dialog has no padding and the panel fills it, so only a press and release that both land on the backdrop count. [`src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx:145`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/b9ffb76f7673fe33ddc04617d587557e93084f96/src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx#L145)
- The close × is a native &lt;button&gt; with an accessible name (closeLabel, default "Close"); its icon is aria-hidden. [`src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx:170`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/b9ffb76f7673fe33ddc04617d587557e93084f96/src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx#L170)
- The sheet grab handle is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. [`src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx:154`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/b9ffb76f7673fe33ddc04617d587557e93084f96/src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx#L154)
- The Content rows are a description list (&lt;dl&gt;, &lt;dt&gt;, &lt;dd&gt;), so each label stays paired with its value. [`src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx:179`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/b9ffb76f7673fe33ddc04617d587557e93084f96/src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx#L179)

### Gaps no ruling covers

The 2026-10-07 ruling leaves these to a human ("Not ruled"). They are open items, not waived.

**Close target.** The close × is `20px` wide and `20px` high ([`Modal.css:124`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/b9ffb76f7673fe33ddc04617d587557e93084f96/src/components/Modal/Modal.css#L124), [`Modal.css:125`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/b9ffb76f7673fe33ddc04617d587557e93084f96/src/components/Modal/Modal.css#L125)), below the 24px minimum of WCAG 2.2 AA (success criterion 2.5.8). The size is a Figma value the 2026-10-07 ruling accepts for the stylesheet gate; the ruling does not cover the accessibility shortfall.

From the [release review](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/141d9ba1e022de1c81ea3b283ea15885bc769d17/reports/Modal/release-review.md), under "Warnings", as written:

> Close target is 20x20 px (Modal.css:124-125), under the 24x24 px minimum of WCAG 2.2 AA success criterion 2.5.8. The size itself is accepted for gate 2 by the ruling, but the accessibility shortfall is not. [designer, then engineer; a human settles]

**Initial focus.** `Modal.tsx` sets no `autoFocus` and calls no `focus()`, so initial focus is left to the browser's `showModal()` ([`Modal.tsx:115`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/b9ffb76f7673fe33ddc04617d587557e93084f96/src/components/Modal/Modal.tsx#L115)); QA and the release review record it landing on the close ×. For the destructive tone, focus on Cancel is the usual practice. Figma draws no focus behaviour.

From the [release review](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/141d9ba1e022de1c81ea3b283ea15885bc769d17/reports/Modal/release-review.md), under "Warnings", as written:

> Initial focus lands on the close button. For the destructive tone, focusing Cancel first is the usual practice, so a stray Enter or Space does not confirm a deletion. Figma draws no focus behaviour. [designer to specify, then engineer]

**No `alertdialog` role.** The root is a plain `<dialog>` for both tones; `alertdialog` appears nowhere in `Modal.tsx`.

From the [release review](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/141d9ba1e022de1c81ea3b283ea15885bc769d17/reports/Modal/release-review.md), under "Warnings", as written:

## Design gaps recorded against it

**Drop shadow.** Figma draws `0 8 12` at 20% black on the four variants (unbound); the panel uses `--elevation-lg` ([`Modal.css:45`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/b9ffb76f7673fe33ddc04617d587557e93084f96/src/components/Modal/Modal.css#L45)), which the token build gives as `0 8 16` at 16% navy. From the 2026-10-07 ruling in [decisions.md](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/b9ffb76f7673fe33ddc04617d587557e93084f96/decisions.md), as written:

> The panel shadow in Modal.css stays on the nearest token, --elevation-lg (0 8 16 at 16% navy), against Figma's 0 8 12 at 20% black on nodes 243:3, 243:49, 243:93 and 243:137. This deviation is accepted as a design decision (2026-10-07), the Figma nodes were not changed, and the QA report (reports/Modal/qa-report.md, round 2) records it.

From the [release review](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/141d9ba1e022de1c81ea3b283ea15885bc769d17/reports/Modal/release-review.md), under "Warnings", as written:

> Shadow differs from Figma (accepted by ruling): code --elevation-lg 0 8 16 at 16% navy against Figma 0 8 12 at 20% black, unbound. If the designer binds the Figma shadow to a token, re-test it against the node. [designer]

**The open sheet spans the viewport.** On screen the sheet is `width: 100%` ([`Modal.css:64`](https://github.com/mavismon/horizon-design-system/blob/b9ffb76f7673fe33ddc04617d587557e93084f96/src/components/Modal/Modal.css#L64)), so on a desktop viewport it is as wide as the window. Figma draws only the 390px sheet, with no desktop width, so there is nothing to check it against. Named in the ruling's "Not ruled" line.
